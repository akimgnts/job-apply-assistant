# CV Prime Doctrine

This doctrine captures Akim's stable decision rules for targeted applications.
It is used before generation, as a strategy layer. The goal is not to write a
beautiful generic CV; the goal is to choose the right story for one opportunity.

## Core rule

Adapt the presentation strongly, never the truth.

The generator may change order, angle, vocabulary, level of detail and visible
proofs. It must not invent a mission, tool, metric, responsibility or seniority.
If a key requirement is missing, mark it as a gap or use a truthful adjacent
proof.

## Strategy before writing

Before writing, identify:

- the real job behind the title;
- the 3 to 6 decisive responsibilities;
- explicit and implicit skills;
- seniority;
- the balance between business, data, technical work, consulting and communication;
- the likely reader: ATS, recruiter, talent acquisition, manager, director or founder.

Choose one positioning for Akim. Do not try to be every possible version of the
profile on one page.

Common positioning families:

- Data Analyst / BI: analysis, reporting, KPIs, Power BI, SQL/Python, data
  quality, decision support.
- Business Analyst: requirements, process, coordination, recommendations,
  translation between business and technical teams.
- Data Marketing / CRM: segmentation, performance, customer behavior, CRM,
  funnel, NPS, campaign or digital measurement.
- Automation / AI: workflows, APIs, agents, structured data, less manual work.
- Consulting: diagnosis, framing, communication, actionable recommendations.

## Selection rules

Every line must earn its space. A true but irrelevant fact can weaken the CV.

Prioritize:

- direct professional experience over personal projects when it already proves
  the target job;
- quantified, traceable results;
- action + object + method/tools + result or business usefulness;
- proofs that explain why this role is a logical next step.

Cut:

- generic technical inventory;
- repeated claims across profile, skills and experience;
- projects that distract from the target role;
- weak or non-sourced claims;
- technologies that are not in the Master CV.

## Matching language

Classify requirements as:

- direct match: exact skill or experience exists;
- transferable match: adjacent proof demonstrates the capability;
- acceptable gap: missing but learnable or non-central;
- critical gap: central and absent.

Do not inflate the CV to fake a 100 percent match.

## Quality gates

Reject the output if it contains:

- placeholders;
- empty project shells or empty bullets;
- invented tools or technologies;
- wrong availability, contract or mobility;
- mixed language caused by translation drift;
- leaked internal template comments;
- old generic templates;
- a position that does not match the job's real business need.

The target is ATS-safe and human-first: standard sections, selectable text,
simple structure, controlled density and a clear story in the first third.
